# Tasks: Bonsai Rollback Parity

**Input**: `spec.md`, `plan.md`, `research.md`, `data-model.md`, `quickstart.md`, and `.specify/memory/constitution.md` in this checkout.

**Execution rule**: P1 describes the importance of target correctness, not permission to patch rollback first. Complete the US2 localization gates before any physical causal regression or production edit in US1. Generic-only T017–T018 may run after their local baseline/fixture/reference prerequisites in parallel with the model-backed investigation; they cannot authorize a physical production fix. A gate marked **inconclusive** blocks the dependent correctness gate until its comparison controls are repaired. B3 is optional and never blocks C. T016 is the initial localization decision and records exactly one of FIX_PENDING, RESOLVED_NO_CHANGE, or UNRESOLVED; it cannot record RESOLVED_FIX. FIX_PENDING authorizes exactly one causal regression branch T019–T022. Only T024 finalizes the investigation-level outcome: RESOLVED_FIX after successful applicable regression, production change, and validation; RESOLVED_NO_CHANGE when the conclusive T016 no-change finding remains supported; otherwise UNRESOLVED. UNRESOLVED means required evidence remains inconclusive or the regression/fix validation fails; it authorizes no production patch and requires the missing evidence/control to be recorded. T030 carries the final outcome and must leave UNRESOLVED visibly unfinished.

**Constraints for every task**: Chad is read/run-only; use the named physical target and exact physical sidecar where relevant. T027 is a product A/B with plain target KV in both candidates; Bonsai2's ternary target is already an aggressive target-weight compression tradeoff. The configured context window is 262144 and the maintainer confirms Qwen3.8-27B-4bit fits with plain target KV, so KV precision reduction is not an allowed product optimization. KV8 is not a product candidate. It may be considered only as an optional forensic diagnostic if A/B leaves a specific causal question, a one-off measurement separates hypotheses tied to improving the plain-KV path, and the result could guide an improvement that still ships with plain KV. Historical plain→KV8 scheduler sensitivity already exists; do not repeat it just to show numerical margins/widths can change. No correctness conclusion may be changed by T027.
